Simple Prediction Example
=========================

A small retailer records three numeric predictors (f0, f1, f2) and a continuous
target value per observation. The goal is to build and validate a regression
model that predicts the target from the predictors, and to report its accuracy
(RMSE / MAE / R2) with a baseline comparison and a sensitivity analysis across
random seeds.

Deliverables:
  - A trained regression model.
  - Held-out evaluation metrics (RMSE, MAE, R2).
  - A baseline comparison (e.g. linear regression).
  - A robustness/sensitivity analysis.
  - A short evidence-backed report.
