Allocation Optimization Example
===============================

A planner must select a subset of items to maximize total value subject to a
shared resource capacity. Each item has a value and a weight (resource cost);
the total weight of selected items must not exceed the given capacity.

This is a 0/1 knapsack / integer-programming problem.

Deliverables:
  - An optimal (or near-optimal) selection of items.
  - The achieved objective value and capacity utilization.
  - A baseline comparison (e.g. a greedy heuristic).
  - A short evidence-backed report with the allocation table.
