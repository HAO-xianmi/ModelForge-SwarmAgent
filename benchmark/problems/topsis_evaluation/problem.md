# Regional Water-Resource Sustainability Evaluation (multi-criteria evaluation)

**Category:** evaluation / multi-criteria decision analysis

A provincial agency must rank 12 counties by water-resource sustainability to
allocate a limited modernization budget. For each county the dataset provides
eight indicators: per-capita water availability, irrigation efficiency, crop
water productivity, groundwater-depletion rate, drought-frequency index,
canal-loss ratio, reuse ratio, and an ecological-flow compliance score. Some
indicators are benefit-type (higher is better) and some are cost-type.

**Q1.** Construct an objective indicator-weighting scheme (do not assign weights
by hand) and justify it.

**Q2.** Build a composite evaluation model that produces a defensible full ranking
of the 12 counties, handling the benefit/cost distinction and normalization.

**Q3.** Perform a sensitivity analysis: how stable is the ranking to perturbations
in the weights and to dropping each indicator in turn? Identify which counties are
robustly top/bottom and which are rank-unstable.

**Q4.** Recommend a budget allocation and explain how the evaluation supports it.

**Deliverable.** A competition paper with assumptions, a symbol table, the
weighting and ranking models with formulas, a validation/consistency check, a
weight- and indicator-sensitivity analysis (table or curves), ranked-result
tables and figures, and a strengths/weaknesses discussion.
